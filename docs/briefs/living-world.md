# Everrealm: Living World and Player Immersion Framework (the owner's master specification)

Saved 2026-11-12. The owner: "do this prompt last since it's refining existing systems and adding to a lot of them."
And: "treat this as a master development specification, not permission to immediately implement everything. Start
with Milestone 1 and make sure NPC schedules, relationships, and memory actually work before layering the other
systems on top." The full text is in the session transcript of 2026-11-12; the 22 systems and the milestones:

1. Living lives: NPC simulation (identity, occupation, home, schedule, family, friends, preferences, traits,
   economy, relationships, memories, activities, goals; NPC-to-NPC relationships; life events; importance tiers).
2. World memory and information propagation (witnesses, rumours, merchants, travellers, guards, letters).
3. Regional culture (customs, festivals, clothing, food, economy, politics, religion; Verandah a wealthy port).
4. Personal legacy: earned titles and character history that NPCs recognise.
5. Social relationships with NPCs: friendship, trust, rivalry, loyalty, favours, invitations.
6. Adventurer immersion: sitting, campfires, eating, inns, sleeping, reading, music, taverns, festivals, with benefits.
7. Dynamic conversations: NPC-to-NPC talk the player can join, overhear and use.
8. Regional events: scheduled and emergent, with participants, outcomes and lasting consequences.
9. Crime and witnesses: theft, pickpocketing, trespass, assault, murder, damage, breaking in, bribery; regional.
10. Supply and demand economy between settlements.
11. Player-owned businesses with stock, staff, costs, reputation, upgrades, customers.
12. Artisan identity: maker's marks, materials, quality, history.
13. Discoverer's journal: places, ruins, legends, wildlife, resources, people, books, mysteries, rumours.
14. Environmental storytelling, reusable.
15. Weather and seasonal consequences.
16. Political influence.
17. Family and generational simulation for important families.
18. Expeditions.
19. The Realm Chronicle: official events, rumours, historical accounts.
20. World requests from real needs.
21. Mount bonding.
22. World simulation architecture: active, background and settlement tiers; event-driven; scalable.

Rules: inspect the codebase first and write the assessment and plan; extend, don't duplicate; no placeholders; save
compatible; server-authoritative where multiplayer; manageable stages; test each. Milestones: (1) schedules,
relationships, life simulation, world memory; (2) conversations, culture, regional events; (3) legacy, social,
crime, immersion; (4) supply and demand, businesses, artisans; (5) journal, environmental storytelling, weather;
(6) politics, families, expeditions, chronicle; (7) requests, mount bonding, optimisation, integration.
