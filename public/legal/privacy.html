Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Everrealm collects as little about you as it can, and sells none of it to anyone.

What we keep

  • Your account: the account name you choose, and your password stored only as a salted scrypt hash, which can't be turned back into the password.
  • Your characters: their names, looks, progress and items, so you can play on any device.
  • Store purchases: which items your account owns, and Stripe's order number for each, so we can deliver and refund them.
  • Server logs: when players connect, with their IP address, kept by our host for a short time to keep the realm running and stop abuse.

What we don't keep

  • Your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Chat. It's passed between players while you're online and not stored.
  • Tracking. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your browser remembers that you're signed in, and that's all.

Who holds it

The game is served by GitHub Pages. The realm server runs on Render, with a backup in Upstash. Payments go through Stripe. Each keeps your data only to run its part.

Deleting your data

You can delete a character from the character screen. To delete your whole account and everything with it, write to support@everrealm.quest and we'll do it within 30 days.
