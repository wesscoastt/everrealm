> add a questline where you break someone out of prison named Daejon Love, after a good amount of questing they become a companion. bioware style writing.

## How it was built

- The quest line is `data/content/daejon.ts` ("The Red Lamp"); he joins as a companion (`data/companions.ts` `daejon`).
- Daejon is on Skua Rock (the Charter's prison off Gullmouth), sentenced for the wreck of the *Constance*: found on Awschitz Head with a lamp in his hand the night she went onto the rocks. He'd run with the Black Gull wreckers at nineteen; that night he lit a red lamp to turn her away. The man who gave evidence against him is Aldous Penberthy, Gullmouth's harbour reeve, who ran the Gull's shore lamps.
- His sister Kezia, on Gullmouth quay, starts it. The red glass is still in the gorse on Awschitz Head. Captain Morvah believes it, can't reopen the case against a reeve on a piece of glass, and won't chase a man off the Rock for a day or two. The breakout goes by the old way off the Rock: the roof, the pipe, the gully, a float in the water, ashore on the dunes north of Gullmouth, with turnkeys landing behind him.
- Then he hides on the Mewstones, and the "good amount of questing": eight more quests done anywhere before he sends word. Penberthy's own ledger in the harbour office clears him; what you do with it is a choice (Morvah and the law, Daejon's say, or Penberthy's purse), and Daejon joins either way, liking you more or less for it.
