# Farming and gardening, in depth

Owner, 2026-11-14, word for word: "farming and gardening should be in depth like cozy games"

## What there is already (2026-11-14)

- Tilled tiles round your home and on the towns' public fields (game/HomeFarm.ts), a seed each, growing on the real
  clock, faster wet than dry; cans of three reaches; rain waters it all; compost for more; a scythe; a group plot.
- 37 crops (data/farming.ts, data/farmingMore.ts) with a season each that grows them half as fast again.
- Orchard patches, a tree each (game/Farming.ts). A scarecrow, a coop and a cow at home. Bertram's three orders a day.
- Friends as farm hands on your land (server/gardens.mjs).

## What cozy games have that this doesn't, and how each goes in

Stardew Valley, Story of Seasons, Animal Crossing, Harvest Moon and Palia, the parts that make a garden a place you
care about rather than a timer:

1. **Quality.** Every harvest comes up plain, good (one star), fine (two) or prize (three), from how well the tile
   was kept: watered every time it dried, composted, weeded, in season, fertilised. Better quality sells and cooks
   for more, and the orders sometimes ask for it.
2. **Soil.** Each tile has its own soil that remembers: worked well it gets richer (darker, more yield), cropped
   with the same family year on year it tires (rotate root, leaf, grain, legume). Peas and beans put it back.
3. **Fertilisers.** Bone meal (quality), wood ash (speed), seaweed (holds the wet), and the compost heap at home
   that turns kitchen scraps, weeds and dung into compost over a day.
4. **Watering you can see.** Wet soil dark, dry soil pale and cracked; plants droop when dry. Sprinklers, made at the
   bench from copper, iron and brass, water the tiles round them every morning.
5. **Crops that regrow.** Strawberries, beans, tomatoes, peppers and raspberries keep bearing after the first pick;
   trees take a real season to come into fruit and then give every few days.
6. **Giant crops.** A three-by-three of pumpkins, marrows or cabbages, all prize, now and then joins into one giant
   one you need a group to cut (co-op), and it goes to the show.
7. **A greenhouse.** Bought and raised beside your home: anything grows in it whatever the season.
8. **Flowers and bees.** Flowers to plant for colour, crossed for new colours (Animal Crossing's hybrids); a hive
   that makes honey flavoured by the flowers near it.
9. **Animals with hearts.** Hens, a cow, sheep, goats and pigs, each named, fed, brushed and let out; their
   affection grows the size and quality of eggs, milk and wool. A barn and a coop as buildings, bought and raised.
10. **Things to make.** A cheese press, a churn, a loom, a preserves jar, a cider press, a keg: produce becomes
    goods worth more, on the real clock.
11. **The show.** A harvest show each season at Hollin Ford: bring your best, judged by quality and size, ribbons
    and a cup on your mantel. Groups enter together.
12. **The look of it.** Paths, fences, gates, trellises, scarecrows of your own design, lanterns, a garden bench:
    a garden that's pretty, the way cozy games' farms are a picture.
13. **A farming journal.** What you've grown, your best of each, the crosses you've found, the animals' hearts.

## Order to build in

Quality and soil first (they touch every harvest), then the visible watering and sprinklers, regrowing crops,
fertilisers and the compost heap, then animals with hearts and the makers, then flowers, bees and the greenhouse,
then giant crops and the show. Each shipped and tested in game before the next.
