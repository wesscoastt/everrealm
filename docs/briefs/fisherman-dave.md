EVERREALM.QUEST — FISHERMAN DAVE & BECKY WHITE
Complete Story Summary, Questline, Dialogue, Character Design, Gameplay Implementation, and World Placement
PART 1 — ORIGINAL STORY SUMMARY AND CREATIVE INTENT
1. The story
This questline introduces Fisherman Dave and Becky White into EverRealm.quest.

The story begins when the player encounters a mysterious ghost in the original quest town. Her identity is initially hidden. She is holding a green object that the player does not yet recognize as Fisherman Dave’s hat.

The ghost is Becky White. She is dead, and the object she carries is a reminder of the man she loved: Fisherman Dave.

After encountering her, the player speaks with Town-Guardian Ford T. Justin. Ford has heard reports of a strange figure moving around town and believes he may have seen something similar himself. He suspects the figure could be Becky, but cannot confirm it. Because Ford is responsible for guarding a particular area and cannot abandon his post, he sends the player to speak with Fisherman Dave.

The player finds Dave at his home. Dave is poor, friendly, eccentric, and heavily intoxicated. He has been drinking since Becky’s death and has become a very different person from the capable individual he once was.

At first, Dave seems genuinely crazy. He misunderstands the player’s intentions, makes strange assumptions, and reacts unpredictably. When the player mentions Becky, he becomes angry and upset. After the player explains that they are only trying to help, Dave calms down and agrees to investigate. However, he admits that he has lost much of his former combat ability and asks the player to help train him.

Dave becomes the player’s companion at this point.

The first companion mission is simple: defeat 15 enemies while Dave is equipped as the player’s companion. These enemies can be anywhere in the existing world. After the training, Dave begins to feel a little more like himself. He is not completely back to normal, but he feels stronger and wants to celebrate by fishing.

The next objective is to catch five fish with Dave.

After fishing, the player and Dave visit Walter White, Becky’s father. Walter misses Becky, despite the differences they had. He explains that if Becky really is a ghost, something darker and more powerful may be involved. He suggests that the player will need something to assist them. He then produces an unusual gift: exactly four pieces of bread and 17 minnows. He questions why the player is looking at him and explains that the minnows can be cooked and eaten.

The player and Dave then search the woods for Becky.

They eventually find her performing a ritual on someone. Becky notices the player, but she is especially surprised to see Dave. The person she was performing the ritual on escapes while she is distracted.

Dave is ecstatic to see Becky. He notices his hat and tells her that he needs it to survive. Becky explains that she took it because she missed him and wanted to keep a part of him with her.

She tells Dave that there is no way for her to return to life and that he needs to move on without her. She disappears. Dave refuses to give up and insists that they speak with Walter about what they witnessed.

After hearing what happened, Walter sends the player and Dave to Ford T. Justin.

Ford remembers a story about a magical Pilgrim Hat that has not been used for hundreds of years. He explains that he always expected he might eventually have to give Dave its location, particularly if an emergency threatened the town. Ford believes Dave will eventually become a powerful being whose abilities may be needed to protect the town. He normally would not reveal the location so casually, but believes Dave deserves this favor.

The player travels to the hidden location and retrieves the Magical Pilgrim Hat. The hat becomes Fisherman Dave’s. It grants him spellcasting abilities and boosts his attack and defense. These abilities belong to the hat, not to Dave naturally.

On the journey back toward town, the player encounters the Cursekeeper. This is the enemy they have been trying to find. The Cursekeeper knows what the player and Dave intend to do and has kidnapped Becky.

The player and Fisherman Dave fight the Cursekeeper together in an epic boss battle.

After the Cursekeeper is defeated, Becky is freed. The curses affecting her are reversed. She is no longer a ghost, no longer undead, and no longer a witch. She becomes human and can live a normal life again.

She is overjoyed to see Dave and excitedly tells him that she is human again. She thanks him, tells him she loves him, and the two are reunited.

Becky remains in the original quest town permanently. The player can interact with her after completing the quest, and she is always happy to speak with Dave.

That is the central storyline. Keep its events and emotional direction intact.

2. Character background
The following information explains the characters and their history. It is provided to help with characterization and consistency. It does not mean every detail should be revealed during this quest.

Becky White
Becky White has been spiritually connected to the supernatural since she was young.

In the current storyline, she is approximately 33 years old in appearance and history, but she died during her mortal life at age 26. She currently exists as a ghost or undead spirit.

She is the former lover of Fisherman Dave. They did not know each other for very long, which makes their story especially tragic. Despite the short amount of time they had together, they genuinely loved one another.

Her father, Walter White, was once a celebrated heroic spellcaster.

When Becky was young, Walter was famous for using magic to help people. He watered crops during droughts, protected villagers from human and animal threats, helped the sick, and built homes for the poor.

However, Walter eventually became obsessed with power. His pursuit of becoming the most powerful spellcaster led him into dark magic.

When Becky was approximately five years old, Walter encountered a witch who gave him a false contract enforced through dark magic. The witch concealed the true contents of the contract with another spell.

The hidden terms made Becky a witch and forced her into the service of a witch clan. Her new existence involved sacrificing bodies for the clan.

The contract also cursed the entire family: they could never find love without being sentenced to death in the name of magic. The witches feared that the family might eventually produce descendants more powerful than they were.

The curse also explains the tragedy involving Becky’s son, Timothy. Becky was forced into a situation where the child would be sacrificed or she would die. The curse affected her mind and behavior, making her think and act like a witch.

This is background lore. Do not turn the Fisherman Dave quest into a full explanation of the witch clan, Timothy, or Walter’s entire past. The quest should focus on Dave, Becky, the Cursekeeper, and their reunion.

The player should discover enough to understand the current story without being overwhelmed by unrelated history.

Appearance: New reference images will be supplied for Becky White in both her ghost form and her restored human form. Use those references closely. Do not invent a completely different appearance.

Walter White
Walter’s history has three major phases:

A heroic and respected spellcaster.
An increasingly power-obsessed dark magic practitioner.
A man who abandoned spellcasting and became a full-time rancher.
Walter originally used magic to help people. His heroic accomplishments made him famous, and he was expected to be remembered in history books as a legendary spellcaster.

Over time, however, his accomplishments were no longer enough for him. He wanted to reach the maximum possible level of power. He described his ambition as wanting to become “pure magic.”

He began experimenting with dark magic. As his power increased, so did his obsession. Eventually, the dark magic began influencing his decisions so heavily that it seemed to be making decisions for him.

Walter wanted to become the most powerful king in existence. He was willing to do almost anything to achieve that goal.

During this pursuit, he encountered the witch who gave him the false contract that ultimately cursed Becky and his family.

Walter eventually abandoned spellcasting completely and became a full-time rancher.

He is still alive during the Fisherman Dave quest. He misses Becky, even though they had their differences.

His personality should be eccentric, unusual, and naturally funny. He should sometimes say things that are unexpectedly strange, but he must still feel like a person with a complicated past and genuine feelings.

His past as a spellcaster should give his dialogue weight. He should not be portrayed as a generic old wizard.

The full origin of the witch and the contract belongs to a different story. Do not make that the central plot of this quest.

Fisherman Dave
Fisherman Dave is poor, friendly, strange, and somewhat unhinged.

He is a gentle but unpredictable person. He can be psycho in his mannerisms without being cruel or malicious.

He is heavily intoxicated during the early part of the story because of Becky’s death. His drinking has affected his confidence, judgment, and ability to fight.

He is motivated by finding Becky again.

He is a man of simplicity and complexity. His unusual personality should be funny, but his grief should be genuine.

Becky took his hat because she missed him and wanted to keep a part of him with her. Dave does not feel like himself without it.

His hat is extremely important to his eventual transformation. However, the magical combat abilities come from the Magical Pilgrim Hat he receives later in the quest, not from an innate ability to cast spells.

Appearance: A reference image will be supplied for Fisherman Dave. Use it closely when remodeling his character. The existing in-game appearance was created by the user’s friend. Do not replace it with an unrelated generic fisherman design.

The Cursekeeper
The Cursekeeper is the main villain of this storyline.

He is not the witch who originally cursed Becky.

In Becky’s universe, Cursekeepers are powerful spiritual beings who ensure that curses remain applied. Their purpose is to prevent curses from being reversed.

Becky has encountered this Cursekeeper before and has even tried to kill him. She was unable to defeat him. Her existence as a dead or undead spirit severely limits what she can affect, and she remains bound to the curse that forces her to serve the witches.

The Cursekeeper is highly connected to magic and curses. He is powerful enough to maintain curses that would otherwise be difficult or impossible to reverse.

There are multiple Cursekeepers and a leader, but the leader does not need to be introduced or discussed in this quest.

The Cursekeeper’s defeat is what allows Becky’s curses to be reversed.

Do not confuse the Cursekeeper with the original witch. Do not make the witch the final boss. Do not introduce the Cursekeeper’s leader as an unnecessary additional villain.
