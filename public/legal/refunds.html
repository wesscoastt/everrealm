Refund policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Within 14 days of buying, you can have a full refund for any reason. Write to support@everrealm.quest from any address with your account name and what you bought. We'll refund the full price to the card you paid with, and remove the item from your account.

How long it takes

We send the refund within two working days of your message. Your bank then usually takes 5 to 10 days to show it.

After 14 days

After 14 days, purchases are final. But if an item didn't arrive or doesn't work as described, write to us at any time and we'll put it right or refund it.

Bought by mistake, or by someone else on your card

Write to us before going to your bank. We'll refund it straight away, with no questions. A chargeback through your bank also works, but it takes longer, and the item is removed and the account paused while it's open.

Free items

Free items, like the Speckled hen, cost nothing, so there's nothing to refund.
