# Continent 3: the elves arrive (owner's brief, 2026-11-27)

The owner's words, verbatim:

> lets have continent 3 unlock a new playable race and have a elven race come into the land from another planet in a sci fi style ship and have it tye into the story and be a world event when continent 3 launches

> elven race being the new race

## What that asks for

- **A new playable race, the elves, unlocked by continent 3 (Serra).** Today every character is human. The elves become the first race you can choose besides them. The creator, the doll, armour fit and the look all need a second body type.
- **Not of this world.** The elves come to the realm from another planet, in a ship built in a science-fiction style. That's a deliberate contrast with the realm's late-medieval look. The ship and its people should feel strange and beautiful, not just shiny, and the story has to earn it.
- **Tied into the story.** Their arrival is part of the main storyline. Why they came, what they fled or sought, and what the realm's peoples make of them (the Assembly, the Counties, Morrow's old kingdom and its white crystal, which may be the thread) all go in docs/CONTINENT3.md.
- **A world event when continent 3 launches.** The ship comes down over the realm as a launch event that everyone online sees and takes part in: the sky, the descent, the landing site, the first contact.

## How it fits what's already planned

- docs/CONTINENT3.md has Serra's draft. Its enchanted forests have "mystical elf-like people the player helps" (owner, 2026-10-23). This brief makes them the elves from off-world, and playable.
- Content order: continent 3 is last, after Morrow (docs/OWED.md item 17). This brief goes with it: design it into the CONTINENT3.md draft now, and build it when Serra is built.
- The writing rules hold (plain names, no AI tropes), and no real IP. These are our own elves and our own ship. They must not look like any game's or film's.
